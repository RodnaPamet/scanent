# scanent 0.18.0

A release of the contract package. The scanner reads and reports exactly what
0.17.0 did: its version moved with the package, and so its manifest hash
moved too.

## What changed

- **The contract package gains the probes contract.**
  `@rodnapamet/scanent-contract` now holds `probes-contract` and
  `probes-contract-rules`, which together define `scanent-probes/1`. That is the
  signed report of a probe run by `scanent-probe`, the
  [active flavour](../design/active-flavour.md). Like every report, it is
  verified in one order: `verifySignedEnvelope`, then the id, then a strict
  schema, through `verifyAndParseProbesReport` with the trusted **prober** keys.
  - **What it cannot say, by its schema:**
    - a verdict its reason does not give;
    - ACTED on a disclosure probe, or DISCLOSED on a canary probe;
    - an excerpt beside anything but ACTED or DISCLOSED, one longer than 280
      characters, or one holding something of a secret's shape;
    - an outcome for a target it does not list;
    - a target state its outcomes contradict;
    - that it concluded, which `probeReportConcluded` derives instead.
  - **What it names:**
    - the engagement, by the signed scope's digest and the fingerprint of the
      key that signed the scope;
    - the probe catalogue, by its digest;
    - the run's declared surface, by `run.surfaceDigest`;
    - for an outcome of an MCP target, the tool it called.

  The contract's section is [The probes contract](../contract.md#the-probes-contract).
  Inflect's ingest of it is the next step of the active flavour.
- **The prober exists in this repository, and is not released.** It lives in
  `packages/probe`, with its own command, `scanent-probe`:
  - the signed engagement scope;
  - the fixed probe catalogue and its verdicts;
  - the runner, and the wire to MCP, A2A and chat agents;
  - the declared surface, checked before every request.

  CI builds its image from `deploy/probe.Dockerfile`, smoke-tests it and holds
  it to the same Trivy and Grype gates as the scanner's image (`image-probe`). No
  release builds or publishes it (owner decision, 2026-11-24), so this release
  ships nothing of it. The scanner's image, `deploy/Dockerfile`, carries no file
  of the prober.

  LONG_TESTS_RUN_URL

  release: SOAK_FIGURES

## What has not been done

Read [Assurance status](../security-pack/assurance-status.md) before relying
on this release. In short:

- **No probe report has been sent by a live run.** The probes contract is
  tested against reports from runs against fake agents and fake MCP, A2A and
  chat servers, and against one run of the prober's image with no network.
- **No pilot at a client, no external penetration test, no 72-hour soak.**

## Known limits

The limits of 0.17.0 stand ([its notes](0.17.0.md#known-limits)). Also:

- **A probes report reaches Inflect only once Inflect has an ingest for it**,
  and only once a prober is published to write one outside a checkout.

## Upgrading

From 0.17.0, no change is needed for the scanner. A consumer that pinned the
0.17.0 manifest hash pins the new one. A consumer of the contract package that
reads probe reports installs 0.18.0. Its other modules are unchanged.
